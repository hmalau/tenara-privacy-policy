# Tenara — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

## Overview
Tenara helps parents guide their children's YouTube viewing distance. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection
Tenara does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Camera Usage
Tenara uses the front camera solely for real-time face distance detection during YouTube playback sessions. No photos or videos are captured, saved, or transmitted. All processing happens on your device in real time.

## Internet Usage
Tenara uses internet access only for:
- YouTube video playback (via WebView)
- Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)
- Video thumbnail fetching (oEmbed)

No data is sent to Tenara servers. Tenara has no servers.

## Third-Party Services
Tenara does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Children's Privacy
Tenara is designed for parents. It does not collect any information from children.
